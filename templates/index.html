Why CV accuracy and LOO accuracy differ

        

          

            
78.6%

            
5-fold CV Accuracy

          

          

            
68.8%

            
Leave-One-Out Accuracy

          

        

        
Both numbers come from the same 52 quarterly observations (FY2013–FY2025) and the same ensemble — the gap is what you'd expect from a small, highly time-correlated dataset, not a bug. 5-fold CV holds out roughly 10 quarters at a time, so each fold still has a broad mix of Stable/Warning/Slowdown periods to learn from. Leave-One-Out holds out a single quarter, so the model sometimes has to classify a quarter without ever having seen a neighboring quarter from the same episode — a harder, more conservative test. Reporting both, rather than just the flattering one, is deliberate: it's an honest signal of how much this model's accuracy should be trusted on a genuinely new, unprecedented quarter.
